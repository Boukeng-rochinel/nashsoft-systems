## Theme Strategy

Dark sections are used for:
- Hero
- Technology
- Projects
- Major CTA
- Footer

Light sections are used for:
- Services
- Industries
- About
- Technology details
- Contact information

This creates visual rhythm rather than making the
entire website dark.
